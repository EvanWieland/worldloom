## Where I disagree with the plan, after testing

- **Rank 1 of §4.1 ("stop rewarding stillness") is a smaller fix than written.** The gate never rejected a cycle; the
  takes creep. Switching to the boundary measure was right on principle and free, but the drift the user sees (pine7)
  is a regional slow change that no gate definition converts into a good loop — only the take does.
- **Endpoint search rescues duration, not drift.** On every cached failure the drift is monotonic over the whole long
  take, so the only "easier" boundaries are shorter loops. Useful as the fallback ladder's first rung (a better 17 s
  than the take-only cut), not as a way to 30 s.

  proven default; half was one seed with marginally lower drift 4.03 vs 4.77 — not enough to change a default).

  cabin. → The keyframe prompt should start from the user's words (the rewrite adds facts but loses the subject).
- **Lighthouse speed diagnostic**: still unrealistically fast even at 0.5x; reads as at least 200x real speed. → the
  storm water is rendered as a time-lapse by the model, not merely played too fast: a uniform retime is not the
